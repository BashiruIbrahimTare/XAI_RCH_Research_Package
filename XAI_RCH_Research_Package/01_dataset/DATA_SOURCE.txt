Dataset: Pima Indians Diabetes Dataset
Source:  UCI Machine Learning Repository
URL:     https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/34/diabetes
License: Public Domain (Creative Commons CC0)

Original citation:
  Smith, J. W., Everhart, J. E., Dickson, W. C., Knowler, W. C., &
  Johannes, R. S. (1988). Using the ADAP learning algorithm to forecast
  the onset of diabetes mellitus. Proceedings of the Annual Symposium on
  Computer Application in Medical Care, 261-265.

Preprocessing applied in this study:
  - Removed duplicate records (768 -> 282 after deduplication)
  - Replaced physiologically implausible zeros in Glucose, BloodPressure,
    SkinThickness, Insulin, BMI with column-wise median values
